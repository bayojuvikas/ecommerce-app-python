Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

We collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and payment details when you create an account, place an order, or interact with our platform. We also collect browsing data, device information, and IP addresses to improve your experience and secure our services.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is primarily used to process orders, deliver products, handle payments, and communicate with you about your purchases. We may also use your data to personalize product recommendations, send promotional offers (if opted-in), and analyze site usage to enhance our platform's performance.

Data Security

E-comXpress takes data security seriously. All data transmissions are protected using TLS 1.3 protocols, and sensitive data at rest is secured with AES-256 encryption. We are PCI-DSS compliant to ensure your payment information is handled with the highest level of security.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share necessary data with trusted service providers, such as logistics partners for delivery, payment gateways for transaction processing, and analytics platforms, strictly under confidentiality agreements to facilitate our services.

Your Rights

In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can also withdraw consent for data processing at any time through your account settings or by contacting our Data Protection Officer.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact our support team at support@ecomxpress.com.
